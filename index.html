02 — EVERYDAY ACTIONS

Good energy habits
start right here.

You don't need a major change to make a difference. Start with one action and make it part of your routine.

☼LIGHTING

Let the daylight in

Switch off lights in empty rooms and use natural light whenever practical.

TIP 01
⌘DEVICES

Power down when done

Shut down computers and devices when they are not needed for a while.

TIP 02
❄COOLING

Cool responsibly

Use air conditioning thoughtfully and avoid cooling rooms that are not in use.

TIP 03
⏻STANDBY POWER

Unplug the unnecessary

Where safe and appropriate, turn off equipment that does not need to stay on.
